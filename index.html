怎么读这张表How to read this table

质量分只统计模型已测的能力，回答“它会的东西做得好不好”；全能分把不支持的能力按 0 计入，回答“一个模型能覆盖多少场景”；广度是已测出分数的能力数，语种是已评分的非中英语言数（共 40 种），覆盖率是模型支持的能力里已测出结果的比例。每格数字是该能力的分数。同一组内质量分相差 1.8 分以内的归为同一档（A、B、C…），请看档位而不是名次。这个宽度来自实测：内容、克隆、长文和 5 种公共语言共 28 道题每个模型各生成 3 次（换随机种子），质量分是 3 次的平均，“±”是 3 次之间的标准差，档宽取“两个平均值能可靠区分”所需的差距。表现力和副语言两个轴仍是每题一次。“公共5语”是日、韩、西、法、德五种语言的平均分，质量分里的多语种部分用它（不足 3 种时退回全部语种平均），冷门语言的表现只影响“多语种”一格和全能分。Quality score averages only the abilities a model was tested on, answering “how well does it do what it can do”. The all-round score counts unsupported abilities as 0, answering “how many scenarios can one model cover”. Breadth is the number of abilities with a score; Languages is the number of scored non-Chinese/English languages (40 in total); Coverage is the share of supported abilities that have results. Each cell is that ability's score. Models within 1.8 quality points in the same group share a tier (A, B, C…): read the tier, not the rank. That width is measured: 28 items (content, cloning, long text and 5 common languages) were generated 3 times per model with different seeds; the quality score is the 3-take mean, “±” is the standard deviation across takes, and the tier width is the gap needed to tell two means apart reliably. Expressive and paralinguistic axes are still one take per item. “Common 5” is the mean score over Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German; the multilingual part of the quality score uses it (falling back to all languages when fewer than 3), so rare-language performance only affects the Multilingual cell and the all-round score.

模型档案Model profiles

每个模型一张卡：一句话定位、强项（＋）、短板（－）和不支持的能力，都由实测分数自动得出——强项是该能力前 3 或前 25% 且 ≥80 分，短板是低于 60 分，或落在后 25% 且比前 25% 低 8 分以上；情绪、副语言、方言这类证据弱的能力只认极端值（≥90 且前 3，或 <60）。组内按质量分排序。One card per model: a one-line positioning, strengths (＋), weaknesses (－) and unsupported abilities, all derived automatically from measured scores. Strength = top 3 on an ability, or top 25% and ≥80; weakness = below 60, or bottom 25% and 8+ points under the top quarter. Abilities with weak evidence (expressive, paralinguistic, dialect) count only at extremes (≥90 and top 3, or <60). Sorted by quality score within each group.

评测是怎么做的How the benchmark works

出题Items

84 道测试题，每题只归入一个能力维度；每个模型只安排它官方声明支持的题。84 test items, each assigned to exactly one ability; each model only runs items it officially claims to support.

本地生成Local generation

所有模型都在同一台 Mac 上用 MLX 推理，记录音频和耗时（RTF）。Every model runs with MLX on the same Mac; audio and timing (RTF) are recorded.

自动打分Automatic scoring

ASR 转写算错字率，UTMOS 测自然度，两个声纹模型测克隆相似度。ASR transcription gives the error rate, UTMOS rates naturalness, and two speaker models measure cloning similarity.

加权汇总Weighted total

每题打分（采样过的题取 3 次平均）→ 每个能力取平均 → 在模型已测的能力上按权重相加得到质量分，不支持的能力按 0 计入得到全能分。Score each item (mean of 3 takes where sampled) → average per ability → weight the abilities the model was tested on for the quality score; count unsupported abilities as 0 for the all-round score.

质量分 = Σ(已测能力的平均分 × 已评比例 × 权重) ÷ Σ 已测能力的权重Quality = Σ(mean score of tested ability × scored share × weight) ÷ Σ weights of tested abilities
全能分 = Σ(能力平均分 × 应测覆盖率 × 权重)，不支持、未测、待验证的能力计 0All-round = Σ(mean ability score × expected coverage × weight); unsupported, untested and pending abilities count as 0
质量分里，已评比例 = 可计分样本 ÷ 实际跑过的样本，生成失败会扣分，没安排的题不扣分。全能分的覆盖率按“应测题数”算，所以它同时反映质量和能力广度。方言轴按自动核验结果计分。同一模型同一题有多次运行时，只取最后一次成功的记录。In the quality score, scored share = scorable samples ÷ samples actually run: generation failures cost points, items never scheduled do not. The all-round score's coverage is over expected items, so it reflects both quality and breadth. The dialect axis is scored from the automatic verification results. When a model ran the same item several times, only the last successful record is kept.

9 个能力维度与权重The 9 abilities and their weights

能力Ability权重Weight怎么测How it is measured
内容准确Content25%Qwen3-ASR 1.7B 转写后算错误率：中日韩泰等按字（CER），其他语言按词（WER）。Qwen3-ASR 1.7B transcribes the audio and we compute the error rate: per character (CER) for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai; per word (WER) for other languages.
自然音质Naturalness22%UTMOS 自然度预测，加上爆音、噪声底、静音、语速等波形检查。所有模型只在同一批中英核心题（朗读、长文、同语种克隆）上比较。UTMOS naturalness prediction plus waveform checks (clipping, noise floor, silence, speaking rate). All models are compared on the same Chinese/English core items (reading, long text, same-language cloning).
多语种Multilingual12%40 种非中英语言朗读（原 8 种 + 32 种扩充）；每个模型只测它声明支持的语言，分数是这些语言的平均质量；会多少种语言单独列在“语种”一栏。高棉、老挝、缅甸语因 ASR 无法识别暂不计分。Reading in 40 non-Chinese/English languages (8 original + 32 added). Each model is tested only on languages it claims; the score is the mean quality over them, and the number of languages is listed separately. Khmer, Lao and Burmese are not scored because the ASR cannot read them.
方言Dialects3%粤语、四川话、上海话等方言朗读，用声学模型自动核验说的是不是目标方言：Qwen3-ASR 语种识别、Voxlect 方言分区分类器（川/豫/陕/鲁）、MMS-LID 语种识别（吴语、闽南语），粤语需前两者一致。上线前用 24 条普通话朗读方言文本做对照，三个模型误判均不超过 2 条，已知方言样本全部判对。判成普通话的记 0 分，判不准的不计分。工具判不准的几条由人工盲听补判（含模型自己的普通话对照，1 位听者、每条 1 段，证据偏弱）；北京话、天津话与普通话太接近，模型和人耳都分不出，按负责人决定记为通过（不是听辨证据）。Cantonese, Sichuanese, Shanghainese and other dialects, verified automatically by acoustic models: Qwen3-ASR language ID, the Voxlect regional classifier (Sichuan/Henan/Shaanxi/Shandong), and MMS-LID (Wu, Min Nan); Cantonese needs the first two to agree. Before use, 24 Mandarin readings of dialect text served as controls: each of the three classifiers wrongly accepted at most 2, and all known dialect samples were accepted. Audio judged Mandarin scores 0; undecidable audio is unscored. A few cases the tools could not decide were judged by blind listening (including Mandarin controls from the model itself; one listener, one clip each, so weak evidence). Beijing and Tianjin speech is too close to Mandarin for models or ears to separate, so the owner decided to count them as passing (not listening evidence).
音色克隆Cloning17%ECAPA 与 CAM++ 两个声纹模型的相似度平均，再结合可懂度与自然度；任一模型判“不可用”即封顶。Mean similarity from two speaker models (ECAPA and CAM++), combined with intelligibility and naturalness; if either model rules the clone unusable, the score is capped.
表达控制Expressive10%声音设计、情绪、风格迁移、时长控制是否真的生效，需要任务内的直接证据。Whether voice design, emotion, style transfer and duration control actually take effect; requires direct evidence inside the task.
复杂副语Paralinguistic3%唱歌、笑声、叹气、呼吸等非朗读能力，同样需要直接证据。Non-reading abilities such as singing, laughter, sighs and breathing; also requires direct evidence.
段落连续性Long-form5%约 9–28 秒短段落的内容完整与重复检查；分钟级长文尚未测试。Completeness and repetition checks on 9–28 second paragraphs; minute-long text is not yet tested.
RTF效率Speed3%生成耗时 ÷ 音频时长（RTF），越低越快；复用全部正式样本。Generation time ÷ audio duration (RTF); lower is faster. Reuses all formal samples.

目前还不能说明什么What this cannot yet tell you

  • 这是开发集上的工程验证，没有独立测试集和完整盲听；只有内容、克隆、长文和 5 种公共语言做了 3 次采样，表达控制和副语言仍是每题一次。分数是探索性代理，不是正式排名。This is engineering validation on a development set: there is no independent test set or full blind listening. Only content, cloning, long text and 5 common languages were sampled 3 times; expressive control and paralinguistics are one take per item. Scores are exploratory proxies, not an official ranking.
  • 方言、情绪、副语言缺少直接证据时记为“待验证”；长文只测了约 9–28 秒的短段落。Dialect, emotion and paralinguistic samples without direct evidence are marked “pending”; long text was only tested on 9–28 second paragraphs.
  • 音色克隆的声纹阈值只用本项目的少量参考音频校准过；克隆相似度只用声纹模型，不用 Gemini（相关性不足）。The speaker-similarity thresholds for cloning were calibrated on only a few reference clips from this project; cloning similarity uses speaker models only, not Gemini (too weakly correlated).
  • 表达控制和副语言两个轴由 Gemini（gemini-3-flash-preview）判定情绪、笑声等是否出现。它能分清“有情绪”和“没情绪”（抽检 10/10 对 3/10），但分不出高低：有情绪的样本几乎都拿满分，所以这两个轴的分数差距很小，只能参考。原评委 gemini-2.5-flash 已对新 API key 关闭，所有控制样本都已改用这个模型重评。Expressive control and paralinguistics are judged by Gemini (gemini-3-flash-preview), which checks whether emotion, laughter and so on appear. It separates “emotion” from “no emotion” (spot check: 10/10 vs 3/10 correct) but not degrees: samples with emotion almost all score full marks, so score gaps on these two axes are small and indicative only. The original judge gemini-2.5-flash is closed to new API keys, so every control sample was re-judged with this model.
  • 历史 RTF 的计时范围不完全一致，速度对比只能看量级。Historical RTF timings do not all cover the same scope, so speed comparisons are order-of-magnitude only.
  • Kitten TTS 2 是本地移植的 MLX 版（网络与官方 PyTorch 运行时对拍：时长逐帧一致，波形因解码器随机噪声不逐样本一致），只有预设声音，不支持克隆、情绪、方言，所以质量分高但全能分低。Kitten TTS 2 is a local MLX port (checked against the official PyTorch runtime: durations match frame by frame; waveforms differ sample by sample because of decoder noise). It has preset voices only and no cloning, emotion or dialects, so its quality score is high but its all-round score is low.
