试听Listen

两种听法：按题目——同一句话，所有模型从高分到低分排开，最适合横向比；按模型——一个模型的全部样本，按能力分组，可以从最差的听起找短板。Two ways to listen: By item — the same sentence from every model, highest score first, best for side-by-side comparison; By model — all samples of one model grouped by ability, starting from the worst to find weak spots.

分数为该条音频在其所属能力上的自动评分（0–100）。CER/WER 为 ASR 听写错误率，UTMOS 为自然度预测（1–5），声纹为与参考音频的余弦相似度，RTF 为生成耗时 ÷ 音频时长。旧版完整审核页见 试听审核报告。Scores are the automatic rating (0–100) of that clip on its ability. CER/WER is the ASR transcription error rate, UTMOS predicts naturalness (1–5), speaker similarity is the cosine to the reference audio, and RTF is generation time ÷ audio duration.
